# Context pack — Premises (internal)

Colour: neutral grey

## What this is

Improvement work to the group's own building — signage, wall graphics, interior and
brand environment.

**This is internal Swytch Group work, not an Eden Building Works client project.** No
evidence says Eden is treating it as a client job. Classify it as internal unless
explicitly told otherwise, even if Eden staff or capabilities are eventually involved.

This context is not a brand. Nothing here is published externally, so there is no
voice, no search intent, and no posting plan. It exists so premises work can be tracked
and asked about alongside the brand work.

Finished premises work may later become Swytch or Sterling content — but that is a
separate decision made under those brands' rules, with the usual permission and
verification checks.

## Workstreams currently known

**Sterling-facing factory area.** Improving Andy's office and its exterior presentation
so it reads considerably stronger for customer visits, potentially including cladding
the office in the new Sterling branding. This sits alongside the wider idea that
bringing customers through the working factory is itself impressive and strengthens
Sterling's credibility.

Stage: discussed and planned. **No confirmed completion or installation date.**

**Large external Swytch Group sign.** The prior plan was to reuse the specification
previously sent for an Alibaba RFQ and get a local quote from **Etchells in Leicester**,
with an intention to purchase before year-end.

Stage: quotation and procurement. **The exact dimensions, specification, approved
artwork, price, supplier decision and installation date are not held.** Do not recreate
the sign specification from memory — it must come from the original document.

**Wider wall graphics and interior programme.** No complete master scope or schedule
held.

A 5S factory poster exists in historic internal visual work, but that does not make it
part of the current premises programme.

## How to handle premises questions

- **Chasing, collecting quotes, gathering information, photographing progress** —
  Tier 1, her call.
- **Any purchase, supplier commitment, or spend** — Tier 3, ask Kev. He is the MD, he
  is in the office, and this is exactly the kind of decision he can make on the spot.
  Sam is not the right person for it and it should not be parked for three weeks.
- **Anything needing the sign specification or dimensions** — do not reconstruct them.
  Find the original document or wait.
