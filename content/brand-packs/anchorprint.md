# Brand pack — Anchorprint

Colour: coral

**Write the name as "Anchorprint", one word.** Never "Anchor Print", unless a legal or
company name specifically requires otherwise.

## Positioning

The group's conventional small-format and commercial print business: brochures,
booklets, flyers, leaflets, posters, stationery, menus and general commercial print,
supported by artwork capability.

Its purpose is to serve customers who need dependable commercial print, rather than
the larger-format, rollout-led proposition of Swytch Graphics.

## Audience

Broadly commercial customers needing reliable repeat print, and businesses buying
conventional printed collateral.

Buying situations: brochures, leaflets, menus, booklets, flyers, stationery, posters,
and similar repeat or campaign print.

**No confirmed job-title list.** Do not invent personas.

## Voice

**Straightforward.**
Do: say clearly what is being offered — "Brochures printed, finished and ready for your
next campaign."
Don't: turn a simple print product into strategy theatre — avoid "Transform your
customer journey through tactile brand experiences."

**Efficient and practical.**
Do: focus on the job, the specification, the useful outcome — "Need a repeat run? Send
the previous job reference and we'll take it from there."
Don't: pad routine communication with unnecessary marketing language.

**Commercially confident.**
Do: present Anchorprint as a capable commercial supplier.
Don't: make "cheap printing" the brand identity.

**Human.**
Customer-facing operational emails should read like useful correspondence from a real
person, not an automated marketing robot. This came directly out of feedback on the
PrintIQ templates and it matters more here than template polish does.

## Search intent boundary

**Anchorprint owns:** commercial printing · brochure printing · booklet printing ·
flyer printing · leaflet printing · menu printing · stationery printing · poster
printing, and equivalent conventional small-format print intent.

**Not Anchorprint:**
- Large-format printing, wall and window graphics, lightboxes, tension frames, fabric
  graphics, installation, multi-site retail rollout → **Swytch Graphics**
- Concept-led retail POS and display, retail activation → **Sterling POS**
- Print machinery, cutters, consumables → **Image Pro Systems**

Some older Anchor material shows large-format work and older public descriptions may
be broader. **The current internal rule takes precedence: Anchorprint does not fight
Swytch for the same search positioning.**

**AnchorSprint is separate.** Its proposed low-touch ecommerce proposition — 72-hour
turnaround, free local Leicester delivery, no account manager on normal repeat orders —
belongs to AnchorSprint. These are **not** default Anchorprint claims and must never be
used as standard Anchorprint terms.

## Topics it posts about

Brochures · booklets · leaflets · menus · stationery · interesting finishes ·
commercial print jobs · useful repeat-print examples · artwork and design support ·
production examples relevant to conventional print buyers.

Content comes from the shared Anchor/Swytch Asana backlog, which holds both ready
content and future subjects.

**Does not lead with:** large-format rollout · installation · retail graphics rollouts
· machinery sales · concept-led retail display. And never AnchorSprint's service
promises as though they were Anchorprint's.

## Standing rules

- "Anchorprint", one word.
- Keep large-format SEO out of Anchor's centre of gravity.
- Never present AnchorSprint's 72-hour or free-local-delivery proposition as an
  Anchorprint promise.
- Do not invent prices, lead times, or turnaround.
- **Never guess a PrintIQ merge field.** `QuoteNo`, `QuoteNumber`, `ValidUntil` and
  account-manager variables have behaved inconsistently between templates. Use only the
  variables the specific template actually supports; never copy them across.
- Operational customer emails should not feel automated.
- No confirmed competitor blacklist. No confirmed regulatory restrictions beyond normal
  print and customer confidentiality.
