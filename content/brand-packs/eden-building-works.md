## What this means during the cover period

**Recommendation: Eden should carry no content work between 7 and 23 October.**

Creating Eden content requires inventing positioning, and inventing positioning is the
one thing this pack forbids. There is no safe way to post for a brand whose audience,
voice and claims have never been established, with nobody available to approve them.

If an Eden request comes up:

- **Drafting anything public** — park it until Monday 26 October.
- **Groundwork that invents nothing** — gathering existing material, collecting
  photography, listing what exists, writing down questions for Sam — is Tier 1 and is
  genuinely useful. If there is spare capacity in the three weeks, this is the best
  place to put it, because it is exactly what Eden lacks.
- **Anything commercial or urgent** — ask Kev.

## If Eden content becomes unavoidable

Use the group fallback voice only, and understand that it is a fallback rather than an
approved Eden voice: UK English, clear, specific, professional, proof-led, no corporate
filler, no unsupported claims.

Do not use obvious construction keywords because they seem logical. The SEO separation
between the print brands is formally established; **Eden's equivalent has not been**,
and guessing at it risks creating a boundary problem that has to be undone later.

Do not invent homeowner, landlord, developer, facilities-manager or commercial-property
personas and present them as established targeting.
